What I do

Web development

I build web-based systems with HTML, CSS, PHP, and MySQL, from the database up to the interfaces that people use. I am eager to work on both the functionality and presentation of a system, ensuring that it is not only functional but also easy to utilize.

UI/UX and graphic design

I design interfaces and visuals using Figma, Canva, Kittl, and Photoshop, focusing on clean and user-friendly layouts. I explore multiple design ideas, and continuously look for ways to make digital experiences more organized, appealing, and easier to navigate.

Database design

I can organize databases using MySQL, from identifying entities and relationships to creating data models that will support a system. I am capable of turning information into structured and manageable data that can work effectively behind an application.

Game development

I create 2D and 3D games in Unity. I am capable of combining my skills with creativity and applying my programming knowledge in order to turn ideas into interactive experiences.

System analysis and documentation

I work on the planning and documentation behind systems, including ERD, DFD, Use Case Diagrams, and other system-related documentation. Organizing ideas and translating system requirements into something that can be clearly understood and developed is something that I am fond of.

Front-end development

I work on the front-end side of applications, focusing on how users interact with and experience a system. I combine HTML, CSS, UI/UX principles, and various design elements in order to create interfaces that are both functional and visually appealing.
